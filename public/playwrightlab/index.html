12

Shadow DOM

Goal: Playwright pierces open shadow roots automatically — normal locators just work here, no special syntax needed.

Open shadow root — normal locators reach inside

Closed shadow root — a genuine hard limit

Built with attachShadow({mode:"closed"}). No Playwright selector, and no other outside script, can reach inside a closed shadow root — the browser doesn't expose it. If you control the source, switch it to open mode; otherwise treat it as untestable from the DOM.
